# Simulated Regulatory Communication Plan

> **SIMULATION ONLY.** No regulator has reviewed this fictional study. No meeting,
> submission or agreement described here has actually occurred.

## 1. Purpose

Demonstrate the information package and questions that a sponsor would prepare before
using a single-arm trial plus external controls in a real development program.

## 2. Simulated communication package

| Item | Simulated content |
|---|---|
| Product | `SIM-MAB-01` |
| Indication | `SIM-DERM-01` |
| Proposed role | Supportive external-control evidence |
| Target trial | Parallel randomized strategy emulated with external controls |
| Estimand | ATT |
| Primary outcome | Week-24 binary response |
| Control sources | One primary and one optional secondary source |
| Key risk | Confounding and transportability between trial and historical sources |

## 3. Questions prepared for simulated scientific advice

1. Is the external-control design acceptable for the proposed evidence role?
2. Is the target-trial alignment of eligibility and time zero adequate?
3. Is ATT the appropriate estimand for the decision context?
4. Are the primary endpoint and phenotype definitions acceptable?
5. Is the primary external source sufficiently comparable to the trial population?
6. What overlap, balance and sensitivity thresholds would be expected?
7. Can the second external source be used as supportive replication only?
8. What source-data access and inspection arrangements are required?
9. What evidence is needed to distinguish drug effect from calendar-time and standard-care drift?
10. What post-authorization or confirmatory evidence would be expected?

## 4. Simulated NMPA/CDE discussion

Prepared topics:

- real-world evidence relevance and reliability;
- source-data applicability;
- cohort construction and exclusion flow;
- estimand and SAP freeze;
- missingness, censoring and immortal-time control;
- multiple source heterogeneity;
- traceability and source-data access;
- boundary between primary, supportive and exploratory evidence.

## 5. Simulated FDA discussion

Prepared topics:

- target-trial emulation;
- external-control comparability;
- positivity and residual confounding;
- sensitivity analyses and quantitative bias analysis;
- reproducibility of statistical code;
- data provenance and audit trail;
- the fact that FDA external-control guidance is not a guarantee of regulatory acceptability.

## 6. Simulated outcome

**Outcome:** no actual advice was requested or received.

The simulation records that regulatory acceptability remains an external human decision.
Passing platform tests cannot establish it.

## 7. Real-study activation gate

A real study may not reuse the simulated “approved” status. It must create a new protocol
and SAP version, document actual advice and approvals, replace all source agreements and
retain regulator access terms that do not conflict with the data-residency model.
